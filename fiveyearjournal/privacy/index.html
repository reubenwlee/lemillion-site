Five Year Journal

Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

The short version

Lemillion LLC makes Five Year Journal. What you write stays on your iPhone. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no server of ours, so we never see your journal. After your first month, the free version shows small ads from Google, which collect some device data to show them. Buying the book once removes the ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your journal entries, and when each one was written.
  • The bookplate: the name, birthday, birth time and nearest city you choose to give, and any signs you set. All of it is optional. It's used on your iPhone only, to choose which short notes the book shows you. The list of cities is part of the app, so nothing is looked up online.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder time and keyboard language.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the journal is part of that backup, which Apple handles under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Ads in the free version

For your first month, Five Year Journal has no ads, and Google's ad code doesn't run. After that you can buy the book once, or keep it free with a small banner from Google AdMob at the foot of some pages. Today's page, where you write, never shows an ad.

  • The app doesn't ask to track you, so Google doesn't get your device's advertising identifier.
  • What Google collects to show and count the ads: your IP address and the rough location it suggests, an identifier for this app on your device, which ads you saw and tapped, and performance and crash data from its ad code. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Where the law requires it, including the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing ads. You can change your answer at any time from the Ads line on the bookplate.
  • Your journal is never shared. Google gets nothing you've written and nothing from the bookplate.

Purchases

The purchase that removes ads is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you own the book, so it can turn the ads off. Once the book is owned, Google's ad code doesn't run.

Reminders and sound

If you set a reminder, it's scheduled on your iPhone; nothing is sent to ring it. The sounds and music are part of the app, so playing them sends nothing to us.

Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

Deleting your data

Deleting Five Year Journal deletes your journal from your iPhone, and it can't be recovered unless you have a backup. Since we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Five Year Journal isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
