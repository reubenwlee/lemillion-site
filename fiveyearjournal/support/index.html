Support

Need help? Email support@lemillion.llc. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Where is my journal kept?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. It's included in your iPhone's own backup, to iCloud or a computer.

How do I move my journal to a new iPhone?

Set up the new iPhone from your old one, or from its backup, and the journal comes with it. Deleting the app deletes the journal, so move it before you delete anything.

Why can't I write on yesterday's page?

Only today's page can be written. At midnight it seals, and the book keeps each day as you left it. Earlier and later pages can be read, but not changed.

Why does an entry stop at 300 characters?

That's about what fits in a paper five-year diary. A few lines a day is the idea: enough to remember the day by.

Why didn't my reminder ring?

Check that notifications are on for Five Year Journal in your iPhone's settings. A day that already has words on its page gets no reminder.

How do I type in another language?

Tap the globe at the bottom left of the keyboard. English, Korean, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and Bengali have their own keys; for Chinese, Japanese, emoji and dictation the book hands over to the iPhone's keyboard.

Why am I seeing ads?

Five Year Journal is free with no ads for your first month. After that, you can own the book with one payment, or keep it free with a small banner at the foot of some pages. Today's page never shows one. To own it, open the bookplate (tap You at the top of any page) and tap Own it.

How do I get my purchase back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the bookplate, tap Own it, then Bought it before? Restore.

How do I change my answer about ads?

Where the law gives you a choice, the bookplate has an Ads line that opens Google's consent form again.
