Lock In

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

The short version

Lemillion LLC makes Lock In. We don't collect your personal data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't send your information to any server we run.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and focus history are stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • The apps, categories and websites you choose to block are stored on your iPhone. Apple's Screen Time gives Lock In coded tokens for your choices, not their names. We never receive them.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Lock In syncs your focus sessions and settings through your own private iCloud account. A synced focus session includes its start and end times, its planned length and whether you completed it. We can't see this data. Apple handles iCloud under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Screen Time

Lock In uses Apple's Screen Time tools only to block the apps you choose during a focus session and unblock them when it ends. We don't use this information for advertising, and we don't share or sell it.

Notifications and sound

Lock In schedules its notifications on your iPhone. Ambient sounds are included in the app or generated on your device, so playing them sends nothing to us.

Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

Links to other sites

The music credits in Settings link to outside websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting Lock In removes its data from your iPhone. To remove synced data, go to your Apple Account's iCloud settings on your iPhone and manage the storage for Lock In. Since we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Anyone can use Lock In, including children under 13. We don't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
