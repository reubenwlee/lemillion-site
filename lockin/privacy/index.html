Lock In

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

The short version

Lemillion LLC makes Lock In. We don't collect your personal data, and the app has no accounts, analytics or tracking. After your first week, the free version shows small ads from Google, which collect some device data to show them. Buying Lock In once removes the ads.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings and focus history are stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • The apps, categories and websites you choose to block are stored on your iPhone. Apple's Screen Time gives Lock In coded tokens for your choices, not their names. We never receive them.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Lock In syncs your focus sessions and settings through your own private iCloud account. A synced focus session includes its start and end times, its planned length and whether you completed it. We can't see this data. Apple handles iCloud under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Screen Time

Lock In uses Apple's Screen Time tools only to block the apps you choose during a focus session and unblock them when it ends. We don't use this information for advertising, and we don't share or sell it.

Ads in the free version

For your first 7 days, Lock In has no ads. After that, if you haven't bought Lock In, a small banner from Google AdMob shows at the bottom of the screen. Ads never show during a focus session.

  • The ads are never personalised. Lock In doesn't ask to track you, so Google doesn't get your device's advertising identifier and doesn't pick ads based on your activity in other apps.
  • What Google collects to show and count the ads: your IP address and the rough location it suggests, an identifier for this app on your device, which ads you saw and tapped, and performance and crash data from its ad code. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Lock In asks for your consent through Google's consent message before showing ads.
  • Screen Time data is never shared. Google gets nothing about the apps you block or how you use your phone.

Purchases

The purchase that removes ads is handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether you own it, so it can turn the ads off.

Notifications and sound

Lock In schedules its notifications on your iPhone. Ambient sounds are included in the app or generated on your device, so playing them sends nothing to us.

Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us crash reports and usage statistics. We use them only to fix problems and improve the app.

Links to other sites

The music credits in Settings link to outside websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Deleting Lock In removes its data from your iPhone. To remove synced data, go to your Apple Account's iCloud settings on your iPhone and manage the storage for Lock In. Since we don't hold your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Anyone can use Lock In, including children under 13. We don't collect personal data from anyone, including children. The ads in the free version are never personalised.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
