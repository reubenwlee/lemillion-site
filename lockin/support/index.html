Support

Need help? Email support@lemillion.llc. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

How do I block apps during focus?

Open Settings in Lock In, tap Allow Screen Time access, then choose your apps. Make sure Block during focus is on. Your iPhone needs a passcode for Screen Time to work.

When do blocked apps open again?

When your focus session ends, even if Lock In is closed. If you end the session early, they open again then too.

Why can't I change my blocked apps?

You can't change them during a focus session. Wait for it to finish or end it, then try again in Settings.

Why am I not getting an alert when focus ends?

Check that notifications are on for Lock In in your iPhone's settings. You can also check the chime and haptics options under Alerts in Lock In's Settings.

Why did the sound stop?

Check that an ambient sound is selected in Settings and that your volume is up. Sounds play during focus, including with the screen locked. After a phone call, a recording starts again from its beginning.

Why isn't my history syncing?

Lock In syncs through iCloud, so make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each iPhone. Settings in Lock In shows whether sync is on. Changes can take a little while to appear.

Does Lock In work on Apple Watch?

Not yet. This version is for iPhone only.
